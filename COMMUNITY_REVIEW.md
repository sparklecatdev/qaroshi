# Qaroshi community language review

Qaroshi contributors should be able to propose vocabulary, grammar, texts and pronunciation without conflating editorial additions with speaker-attested language. Every change should retain the 26 canonical glyphs and the obligatory 16 sentence-initial resonance markers unless there is an explicit standards decision.

## Editorial stages
1. Proposed: entry or rule includes precise meaning and examples but has not been independently reviewed.
2. Checked: morphological validity and source references verified; semantic naturalness still provisional.
3. Approved: two independent reviewers agree on interpretation, contextual examples and backward compatibility.
4. Published: release contains a changelog, traceable decision and regression tests.

## Access and safety
Do not publish private identifying information, recordings without permission or inferred dialect/identity labels. In particular, recordings should only appear publicly after a consent and licensing check. Contributors may choose pseudonyms. A GitHub issue is not a replacement for a private audio collection mechanism.

## Moderation
Critique language claims and examples, not individuals. Clear descriptions of anatomy, sexuality or profanity are allowed as linguistic material with register labels; gratuitous harassment is not. Report conflicting definitions through the grammar or lexeme templates, with minimal pairs and example context.
