# Qaroshi Release 28–34 · Normative language amendment

## Language changes adopted

- New, formally spelled and IPA-transcribed **`nëgëra`**, a nonuniversal quantifier determiner. It distinguishes `∃¬` from universal predicate negation `∀¬`, with a narrowly scoped conservative interpreter and explicit empty-domain requirements.
- Clarified **30 lexical contrasts** within previously established words: grammatical/pragmatic categories, word-sense exclusion and high-impact false-synonym traps.
- Licensed **108 precise corpus-exemplified object constructions** for existing verbs (each exact source clause and object). The previous 25 full verb frames remain authoritative. The other 267 entries in the expanded 400-frame inventory are strictly descriptive and provisional.
- Standardized 20 discourse rules for recoverable fragments, referent tracking, corrections, evidentials, consent and register, without licensing unmarked finite independent sentences.
- Rechecked all 13,379 established canonical modifier–head compounds, flagging digraph-boundary and multi-gloss risk without changing official spellings or counting proposals as new words.
- Classified 32 existing reference literary usages by domain and interpretation constraints. No spontaneous speaker folklore or dialect frequencies invented.

## Language not altered

The 30,000 frozen legacy lexemes, original IDs, 4,383 controlled references, 90 grammar chapters, 16 written resonance particles, 26 glyphs, source books, and 24–27 passive-agent `-za` behavior remain unchanged. The **edition-specific effective lexicon is 30,001** after applying the one new word overlay.

The unapproved 65 v16.2 alternative root proposals remain 44 trials, nine holds, 12 rejections, and zero silently adopted replacements. No arbitrary second general-purpose negator or extra past-tense marker has been added.
