# Qaroshi 15.0: independent editorial conversations and literature

These pieces were separately scripted as new teaching/editorial writing. They are not transcribed speech, not verified native idioms, and not a naturalistic 100,000-word corpus.

## The Quiet Archive
*library · dialogue*
Two archivists decide whether an uncertain report belongs in the public record.

**A: hë ti shiwaro-m shëliro-te he?**
*Did you read the message?*
IPA `/hə ti ʃi.ˈwa.ɾom ʃə.li.ˈɾo.te he/`

**B: sen mi shiwaro-m shëliro-te.**
*I read the message.*
IPA `/sen mi ʃi.ˈwa.ɾom ʃə.li.ˈɾo.te/`

**A: hë ti sa-m tika-te si he?**
*Did you witness them yourself?*
IPA `/hə ti sam ti.ˈka.te si he/`

**B: zher mi sa-m nu tika-te.**
*I did not see them.*
IPA `/ʒeɾ mi sam nu ti.ˈka.te/`

**A: sen mi shiwaro-m shiroli-yu.**
*I will record the message.*
IPA `/sen mi ʃi.ˈwa.ɾom ʃi.ɾo.ˈli.ju/`

**B: qor mi shiwaro-m daru-yu.**
*I will protect the message.*
IPA `/qoɾ mi ʃi.ˈwa.ɾom da.ˈɾu.ju/`
