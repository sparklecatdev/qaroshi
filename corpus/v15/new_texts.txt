# New 15.0 independent editorial continuous texts. Each line is a separate sentence with a contextual English rendering.
@ The Wind and the Witness | short fiction | A traveler discovers that evidence must be distinguished from rumor.
sen mi yami-lo goro-te.|I flew at night.
sen mi gëru-m tika-te.|I saw a mountain.
zher mi sa-m nu tika-te.|I did not see the traveler.
sen sa goro-te hu.|I was told the traveler had flown.
hë ti sa-m tika-te si he?|Did you see them yourself?
sen mi sa-m nu tika-te.|I did not see them.
sen mi shiwaro-m shiroli-te.|I wrote a report.
zher sa goro-te ye.|I infer they flew.
sen mi shiwaro-m shiwari-yu.|I will send a message.
nëm mi naro-lo nëru-yu.|I will rest at home.

@ A School for Questions | educational essay | An inquiry-based education avoids mistaking uncertainty for ignorance.
sen mi qaroshi-m shiri.|I speak Qaroshi.
hë ti qaroshi-m shiri he?|Do you speak Qaroshi?
zher mi shëno-m hari.|I have a question.
hë mi shëno-m sharoa-ho he?|May I ask a question?
sen mi shëno-m sharoa-te.|I asked the question.
sen mi ti-m shiroawe-te.|I listened to you.
sen mi shima-m shëliro-te.|I read the book.
zher mi nu rëmi.|I am not yet learning.
dal mi rëmi-yu.|I will learn.
hol mi ti-m holë.|Thank you.

@ The Archive of Uncertain Stories | archival essay | An archivist declines to promote unverified events into history.
sen mi shima-m shëliro-te.|I read a book.
sen mi moru-m hari.|I have a memory.
sen sa goro-te hu.|Someone reported that they flew.
zher mi sa-m nu tika-te.|I did not see them.
sen mi shiwaro-m shiroli-te.|I wrote a record.
hë ti sa-m tika-te si he?|Did you witness them?
zher sa goro-te ye.|I infer they may have flown.
sen mi shiwaro-m nu shiwari-yu.|I will not send the report.
sen mi shëno-m sharoa-yu.|I will ask a question.
qor mi moru-m daru-yu.|I will protect the memory.

@ The Ethics of a Refusal | philosophical essay | A refusal remains a refusal even when the invitation is affectionate.
hë ti mi-wa goro-yu he?|Will you fly with me?
sen mi nu goro-yu.|I will not fly.
më mi ti-m nashi.|I care deeply about you.
sen mi ti-m ronaro.|I hear you.
hë ti mi-wa nëra-yu he?|Will you stay with me?
sen mi ti-wa nëra-yu.|I will stay with you.
hë ti goro-ki he?|Are you physically able to fly?
sen mi goro-ki.|I can fly.
hë ti goro-ho he?|Are you permitted to fly?
sen mi nu goro-ho.|I am not permitted to fly.

@ One Star, Three Meanings | literary meditation | A night sky offers a setting for different readings of the same object.
sen mi sëra-m tika.|I see a star.
nëm mi yami-lo nëru.|I rest in the night.
më mi sëra-m nashi.|I cherish the star.
hë ti sëra-m hari he?|Do you possess the star?
sen mi sëra-m nu hari.|I do not possess the star.
sen mi moru-m hari.|I have a memory.
zher mi sëra-m tika-yu ye.|I infer that I may see the star again.
sen mi moru-m shiroli-yu.|I will write down a memory.
wen mi sëra-m tika-yu.|I will look at the star with longing.
nëm mi naro-lo nëru-yu.|I will rest at home.

@ The Machine and the Promise | technical reflection | A repair is delayed when an exhausted mechanic sets a boundary.
sen mi shenaro-m hari.|I have a device.
sen mi shenaro-m tika-te.|I inspected the device.
hë ti shenaro-m rëgë-ki he?|Can you repair it?
sen mi shenaro-m rëgë-ki.|I can repair it.
sur mi shërohi.|I am tired.
sen mi shenaro-m nu rëgë-yu.|I will not repair it yet.
hë ti nëru-yu he?|Will you rest?
nëm mi nëru-yu.|I will rest.
qor mi shenaro-m rëgë-yu.|I will repair the device.
hol mi ti-m holë.|Thank you.

@ An Open Door in the Storm | short fiction | A visitor seeks shelter and the host offers practical care.

sen mi lushënga-m tika-te.|I saw the storm.
sur mi shërohi.|I am tired.
sen mi naro-m nu hari.|I have no home.
hë mi naro-gu nëra-ho he?|May I stay at the home?

@ A Letter Across Distance | epistolary fiction | A friend sends one carefully chosen message rather than a grand promise.
