# Five original editorial Qaroshi readings

These are new connected learning passages. They are not independently attested cultural texts. All include standard sentence-initial resonance, IPA, and translations.

## The Home with an Open Door

A visitor finds shelter after a difficult night

**1.** sur mi naro-m nu hari.
*IPA:* `/suɾ mi ˈna.ɾom nu ˈha.ɾi/` · *Sound-out:* soor mee NAH-rohm noo HAH-ree.
*English:* I have no home.

**2.** sen mi yami-lo goro-te.
*IPA:* `/sen mi ja.ˈmi.lo ɡo.ˈɾo.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee yah-MEE-loh goh-ROH-teh.
*English:* I flew in the night.

**3.** sen mi naro-m tika-te.
*IPA:* `/sen mi ˈna.ɾom ti.ˈka.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee NAH-rohm tee-KAH-teh.
*English:* I saw a home.

**4.** hë mi naro-gu nëra-ho he?
*IPA:* `/hə mi na.ˈɾo.ɡu nə.ˈɾa.ho he/` · *Sound-out:* huh mee nah-ROH-goo nuh-RAH-hoh heh?
*English:* May I stay at the home?

**5.** më ti naro-gu nëra-ho.
*IPA:* `/mə ti na.ˈɾo.ɡu nə.ˈɾa.ho/` · *Sound-out:* muh tee nah-ROH-goo nuh-RAH-hoh.
*English:* You may stay at the home.

**6.** sen mi naro-gu nëra-te.
*IPA:* `/sen mi na.ˈɾo.ɡu nə.ˈɾa.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee nah-ROH-goo nuh-RAH-teh.
*English:* I stayed at the home.

**7.** hol mi mara-m nashi.
*IPA:* `/hol mi ˈma.ɾam ˈna.ʃi/` · *Sound-out:* hohl mee MAH-rahm NAH-shee.
*English:* I cherish my friend with gratitude.

**8.** sen mi kima-m këmë-te.
*IPA:* `/sen mi ˈki.mam kə.ˈmə.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee KEE-mahm kuh-MUH-teh.
*English:* I drank water.

**9.** nëm mi nëru-te.
*IPA:* `/nəm mi nə.ˈɾu.te/` · *Sound-out:* nuhm mee nuh-ROO-teh.
*English:* I rested.

**10.** më mi naro-m hari.
*IPA:* `/mə mi ˈna.ɾom ˈha.ɾi/` · *Sound-out:* muh mee NAH-rohm HAH-ree.
*English:* I have a home.

## The Flight We Did Not Take

A weather report changes the course of a planned journey

**1.** zha naru goro-yu.
*IPA:* `/ʒa ˈna.ɾu ɡo.ˈɾo.ju/` · *Sound-out:* zhah NAH-roo goh-ROH-yoo.
*English:* We will fly!

**2.** sen mi lugoqa-m tika-te.
*IPA:* `/sen mi lu.ˈɡo.qam ti.ˈka.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee loo-GOH-qahm tee-KAH-teh.
*English:* I saw the weather.

**3.** sen mi lushënga-m tika-te si.
*IPA:* `/sen mi lu.ˈʃə.ŋam ti.ˈka.te si/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee loo-SHUH-ngahm tee-KAH-teh see.
*English:* I witnessed the storm.

**4.** hë ti lushënga-m tika-te he?
*IPA:* `/hə ti lu.ˈʃə.ŋam ti.ˈka.te he/` · *Sound-out:* huh tee loo-SHUH-ngahm tee-KAH-teh heh?
*English:* Did you see the storm?

**5.** sen mi lushënga-m tika-te hu.
*IPA:* `/sen mi lu.ˈʃə.ŋam ti.ˈka.te hu/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee loo-SHUH-ngahm tee-KAH-teh hoo.
*English:* I heard a report about the storm.

**6.** qor ti nu goro ya.
*IPA:* `/qoɾ ti nu ˈɡo.ɾo ja/` · *Sound-out:* qohr tee noo GOH-roh yah.
*English:* Do not fly.

**7.** zher mi goro-yu ye.
*IPA:* `/ʒeɾ mi ɡo.ˈɾo.ju je/` · *Sound-out:* zhehr mee goh-ROH-yoo yeh.
*English:* I infer that I may fly later.

**8.** nëm mi naro-lo nëra-te.
*IPA:* `/nəm mi na.ˈɾo.lo nə.ˈɾa.te/` · *Sound-out:* nuhm mee nah-ROH-loh nuh-RAH-teh.
*English:* I stayed at home.

**9.** sen mi moru-m shiroli-te.
*IPA:* `/sen mi ˈmo.ɾum ʃi.ɾo.ˈli.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee MOH-room shee-roh-LEE-teh.
*English:* I wrote down the memory.

**10.** dal naru yurayaro goro-yu.
*IPA:* `/dal ˈna.ɾu ju.ɾa.ˈja.ɾo ɡo.ˈɾo.ju/` · *Sound-out:* dahl NAH-roo yoo-rah-YAH-roh goh-ROH-yoo.
*English:* We will fly tomorrow.

## The Message That Returned

Two friends repair a misunderstanding instead of trading blame

**1.** sen mi ti-m shiwaro-m shiwari-te.
*IPA:* `/sen mi tim ʃi.ˈwa.ɾom ʃi.wa.ˈɾi.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee teem shee-WAH-rohm shee-wah-REE-teh.
*English:* I sent you a message.

**2.** hë ti shiwaro-m shëliro-te he?
*IPA:* `/hə ti ʃi.ˈwa.ɾom ʃə.li.ˈɾo.te he/` · *Sound-out:* huh tee shee-WAH-rohm shuh-lee-ROH-teh heh?
*English:* Did you read the message?

**3.** lën mi shiwaro-m nu shëliro-te.
*IPA:* `/lən mi ʃi.ˈwa.ɾom nu ʃə.li.ˈɾo.te/` · *Sound-out:* luhn mee shee-WAH-rohm noo shuh-lee-ROH-teh.
*English:* I did not read the message.

**4.** rak mi shëna-m nu hari.
*IPA:* `/ɾak mi ˈʃə.nam nu ˈha.ɾi/` · *Sound-out:* rahk mee SHUH-nahm noo HAH-ree.
*English:* I do not feel confidence.

**5.** lën mi përë.
*IPA:* `/lən mi ˈpə.ɾə/` · *Sound-out:* luhn mee PUH-ruh.
*English:* I apologize.

**6.** hë ti mi-m shiroawe-ki he?
*IPA:* `/hə ti mim ʃi.ɾo.a.ˈwe.ki he/` · *Sound-out:* huh tee meem shee-roh-ah-WEH-kee heh?
*English:* Can you listen to me?

**7.** sen mi ti-m shiroawe-yu.
*IPA:* `/sen mi tim ʃi.ɾo.a.ˈwe.ju/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee teem shee-roh-ah-WEH-yoo.
*English:* I will listen to you.

**8.** më mi ti-m nashi.
*IPA:* `/mə mi tim ˈna.ʃi/` · *Sound-out:* muh mee teem NAH-shee.
*English:* I care deeply about you.

**9.** sen mi shiwaro-m shëliro-te.
*IPA:* `/sen mi ʃi.ˈwa.ɾom ʃə.li.ˈɾo.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee shee-WAH-rohm shuh-lee-ROH-teh.
*English:* I read the message.

**10.** nëm mi nëru-te.
*IPA:* `/nəm mi nə.ˈɾu.te/` · *Sound-out:* nuhm mee nuh-ROO-teh.
*English:* I rested.

## The Smallest Promise

A promise is phrased narrowly to avoid false certainty

**1.** hë ti mi-wa nëra-yu he?
*IPA:* `/hə ti ˈmi.wa nə.ˈɾa.ju he/` · *Sound-out:* huh tee MEE-wah nuh-RAH-yoo heh?
*English:* Will you stay with me?

**2.** më mi ti-wa nëra-yu.
*IPA:* `/mə mi ˈti.wa nə.ˈɾa.ju/` · *Sound-out:* muh mee TEE-wah nuh-RAH-yoo.
*English:* I will stay with you.

**3.** hë ti sëna nëra-yu he?
*IPA:* `/hə ti ˈsə.na nə.ˈɾa.ju he/` · *Sound-out:* huh tee SUH-nah nuh-RAH-yoo heh?
*English:* Will you stay forever?

**4.** zher mi sëna nëra-ki ye.
*IPA:* `/ʒeɾ mi ˈsə.na nə.ˈɾa.ki je/` · *Sound-out:* zhehr mee SUH-nah nuh-RAH-kee yeh.
*English:* I infer that I might be able to stay forever.

**5.** qor mi nënayaro ti-wa nëra-yu.
*IPA:* `/qoɾ mi nə.na.ˈja.ɾo ˈti.wa nə.ˈɾa.ju/` · *Sound-out:* qohr mee nuh-nah-YAH-roh TEE-wah nuh-RAH-yoo.
*English:* I will stay with you today.

**6.** sen mi namu-m hari.
*IPA:* `/sen mi ˈna.mum ˈha.ɾi/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee NAH-moom HAH-ree.
*English:* I have a promise.

**7.** më mi ti-m nashi.
*IPA:* `/mə mi tim ˈna.ʃi/` · *Sound-out:* muh mee teem NAH-shee.
*English:* I cherish you.

**8.** sen mi ti-m shiroawe-yu.
*IPA:* `/sen mi tim ʃi.ɾo.a.ˈwe.ju/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee teem shee-roh-ah-WEH-yoo.
*English:* I will listen to you.

**9.** nëm naru nëru-yu.
*IPA:* `/nəm ˈna.ɾu nə.ˈɾu.ju/` · *Sound-out:* nuhm NAH-roo nuh-ROO-yoo.
*English:* We will rest.

**10.** hol mi ti-m nashi.
*IPA:* `/hol mi tim ˈna.ʃi/` · *Sound-out:* hohl mee teem NAH-shee.
*English:* I am grateful for you.

## Learning a New Voice

A learner makes progress through questions rather than perfection

**1.** hë ti mi-m yarëmi-ki he?
*IPA:* `/hə ti mim ja.ɾə.ˈmi.ki he/` · *Sound-out:* huh tee meem yah-ruh-MEE-kee heh?
*English:* Can you teach me?

**2.** sen mi ti-m yarëmi-ki.
*IPA:* `/sen mi tim ja.ɾə.ˈmi.ki/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee teem yah-ruh-MEE-kee.
*English:* I can teach you.

**3.** hë mi shëno-m sharoa-ho he?
*IPA:* `/hə mi ˈʃə.nom ʃa.ɾo.ˈa.ho he/` · *Sound-out:* huh mee SHUH-nohm shah-roh-AH-hoh heh?
*English:* May I ask a question?

**4.** më ti shëno-m sharoa-ho.
*IPA:* `/mə ti ˈʃə.nom ʃa.ɾo.ˈa.ho/` · *Sound-out:* muh tee SHUH-nohm shah-roh-AH-hoh.
*English:* You may ask a question.

**5.** sen mi shëno-m sharoa-te.
*IPA:* `/sen mi ˈʃə.nom ʃa.ɾo.ˈa.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee SHUH-nohm shah-roh-AH-teh.
*English:* I asked a question.

**6.** sen ti mi-m përoa-te.
*IPA:* `/sen ti mim pə.ɾo.ˈa.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn tee meem puh-roh-AH-teh.
*English:* You answered me.

**7.** zher mi nu rëmi.
*IPA:* `/ʒeɾ mi nu ˈɾə.mi/` · *Sound-out:* zhehr mee noo RUH-mee.
*English:* I am not learning.

**8.** dal mi rëmi-yu.
*IPA:* `/dal mi ɾə.ˈmi.ju/` · *Sound-out:* dahl mee ruh-MEE-yoo.
*English:* I will learn.

**9.** sen mi shiwaro-m shiroli-te.
*IPA:* `/sen mi ʃi.ˈwa.ɾom ʃi.ɾo.ˈli.te/` · *Sound-out:* sehn mee shee-WAH-rohm shee-roh-LEE-teh.
*English:* I wrote a message.

**10.** rëm mi shiwaro-m shëliro-te.
*IPA:* `/ɾəm mi ʃi.ˈwa.ɾom ʃə.li.ˈɾo.te/` · *Sound-out:* ruhm mee shee-WAH-rohm shuh-lee-ROH-teh.
*English:* I read the message with pride.
