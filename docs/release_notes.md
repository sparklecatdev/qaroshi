# Qaroshi Standard 7.0 · Grammar and Linguistics

## Standard 7.0: Linguistics and grammar expansion

This is the **deep grammar release**, built on the original 30,000-word reference. **All 30,000 dictionary records and all 4,383 original bilingual teaching examples remain untouched.** The first 70 grammar chapters remain in their original order and wording, with 20 explanatory chapters added (71–90).

### New resources

- **9 advanced manuals and workbooks** covering sentence structure, subordinate clauses, scope, tense/modality, conversational discourse, phonemic vs fluent pronunciation, lexical semantics, style/dialects, genre and rigorous translation.
- **301 annotated lines of connected text** in **22 dialogues** and **13 narrative/expository passages**, each with speaker or narrator, resonance, English, IPA, sound-out and an explanation. They are maintained separately from the 4,383 legacy phrasebook examples.
- **94 controlled meaning contrast sets** containing **196 individual study sentences**; these target tense, aspect, modality, negation, evidentials, focus and pronoun reference.
- **25 curated predicate frames**, **26 distinguished word senses**, **32 figurative interpretations**, and **208 phoneme recognition drills** using existing documented words.
- A more comprehensive **advanced workbook** with exercises and answer keys, and an expanded self-contained Wiki with dedicated **Connected texts**, **Grammar lab**, **Semantics & idioms**, and **Sound lab** pages. The Academy includes all 25 deep guides; the existing 16 lessons and eight games remain intact.

**Start:** Open `Qaroshi_Wiki.html` and select **Connected texts** or **Grammar lab** in the sidebar, or open `Qaroshi_Academy.html` and select **Deep guides**.

**Scope and honesty:** These are controlled editorial examples and standardized reference conventions, not independently recorded natural-language data. The connected-text collection is substantially more useful than isolated example rows, but it is not yet a large naturalistic speaker corpus. No new lexical headwords or artificial counts were added.


## Earlier release history

# Qaroshi Standard 5.1 · Deep learning edition

## New in the deep learning edition

- Nineteen integrated learning resources: a 16-lesson progressive course, 16 deep topical guides (including the start guide), a 21-sentence interlinear analysis atlas, and a 46-item editing and translation workbook (ten error diagnoses plus 36 translation questions).
- Every lesson uses ten unchanged corpus examples with IPA and full answer keys. Relevant deep guides appear as links from grammar chapters and the new sidebar Learning section.
- Source guides (translation, writing, pronunciation, lexicography, wiki usage) are substantially longer and feature step-by-step procedures and comparisons.
- Old dictionary words, existing example rows, phoneme inventory, native glyphs, grammar rules and sentence-initial resonance inventory are unchanged.
- All new illustrative sentences in the graded course and atlas are drawn from stable internal corpus references. Deliberately incorrect examples in the editing workbook are explicitly labeled as mistakes.

## Dictionary and corpus

- 16,732 unique dictionary headwords: 11,878 preserved prior terms, 2,122 new standalone items, and 2,732 new transparent compounds.
- 3,077 bilingual sentences, all with a written resonance marker, IPA, stress guide and approachable sound-out.
- Expanded technical, practical, scientific, civic and Nardoragon-specific terminology across 35 new vocabulary domains.
- All 70 grammar chapters and 26 handwritten glyphs retained.

## Offline wiki redesign

- A more polished and restrained wiki visual style with a responsive sidebar and on-page table of contents.
- Homepage with subdued native-glyph artwork, clearer reference statistics and responsive reference cards.
- Faster dictionary lookups through pre-normalized search indices; accent-insensitive search for Romanized Qaroshi.
- Correct example links indexed by whole lexical tokens (avoids false positives from matching substrings).
- Dictionary compound components link directly to their constituent word pages.
- High-visibility English-style pronunciation, exact IPA and stress guides; collapsible letter-sound breakdowns for longer compounds.
- Improved responsive layouts, word wrapping, contrast and keyboard-focus states. No external assets or network dependencies.

## Integrity

Existing 4.3.1 lexical and corpus records are identical and retain their original IDs. Added terms are stable, uniquely spelled, non-inflectional canonical entries. The source code, sample corpus and original writing system are included with the release.

## Limitations

A large lexicon does not imply one-to-one parity with all natural-language idioms, dialects, usage frequencies or registers. A controlled translation example is not spontaneous-speech attestation. Phonetic pronunciation is rule-based, and English sound-outs are approximate.

## Unified Standard 5.2 · Qaroshi Academy

The app includes sixteen guided lessons, eight mini-game modes, a ten-question mixed Skyflight challenge, spaced word review, local progress export/import, native glyph decoding, and a full embedded dictionary and phrasebook with IPA and English-style sound-out. ## Unified Standard 6.0: 30,000-entry Vocabulary Atlas

The complete lexicon contains 30,000 distinct Qaroshi spellings: 18,663 preserved from 5.3, plus 924 new independent bases and 10,413 transparent compounds. Added 1,200 controlled teaching sentences with eight different topical examples. The grammar, original 26 glyphs, 16 written resonance markers and Academy course sequence are preserved. English index and IPA/sound-out fields have been regenerated. Source code for the expansion and validation is bundled. Editorially standardized terminology is not evidence of frequency or independently attested everyday use.
