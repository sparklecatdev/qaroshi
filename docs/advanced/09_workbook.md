# Qaroshi 7.0 advanced workbook

This workbook complements the earlier 16-lesson course and tests grammatical and pragmatic *distinctions*, not only vocabulary recall.
The standard 30,000 dictionary entries, 16 sentence-initial resonances and 26 original glyphs remain unchanged.
Every Qaroshi example has a reproducible IPA and English-oriented sound-out. These are controlled editorial examples, not naturalistic speaker recordings.

## Instructions

For each contrast: (1) translate each sentence, (2) state what the contrast changes, (3) state one interpretation it does **not** justify.
For each context question: read the surrounding turns before choosing an answer. The intended lesson is context and scope.

## Part I: 94 grammatical and semantic contrasts

### 1. fly: past vs future
*Focus:* tense.

1. **`sen mi goro-te.`**
   *IPA:* `/sen mi ɡo.ˈɾo.te/`; *reading:* `sehn mee goh-ROH-teh.`.
2. **`sen mi goro-yu.`**
   *IPA:* `/sen mi ɡo.ˈɾo.ju/`; *reading:* `sehn mee goh-ROH-yoo.`.

<details><summary>Answer and interpretation</summary>

- **1:** I flew.
- **2:** I will fly.
- **Why the contrast matters:** Past -te and future -yu occupy alternative tense slots on goro.

</details>

## Part V: Syntax and editing diagnostics

For each pair identify the better standard form and explain the violated rule. The marked incorrect examples here are **deliberate exercises**, not model phrases.

1. Evaluate: **`ti haro.`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>Complete independent sentences need a resonance. Preferred repair (if any): `sen ti haro.`.</details>

2. Evaluate: **`meë ti haro.`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>Warm Hum is spelled më, not meë. Preferred repair (if any): `më ti haro.`.</details>

3. Evaluate: **`hë ti haro?`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>Direct yes/no questions end with he. Preferred repair (if any): `hë ti haro he?`.</details>

4. Evaluate: **`sen mi kima-m këmë nu.`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>nu immediately precedes its predicate. Preferred repair (if any): `sen mi kima-m nu këmë.`.</details>

5. Evaluate: **`sen mi goro-se-te.`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>Tense precedes aspect. Preferred repair (if any): `sen mi goro-te-se.`.</details>

6. Evaluate: **`sen mi goro-te-yu.`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>One tense slot per finite verb. Preferred repair (if any): `sen mi goro-te.`.</details>

7. Evaluate: **`sen mi goro-ki-ho.`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>One modality per finite verb. Preferred repair (if any): `sen mi goro-ki.`.</details>

8. Evaluate: **`sen mi mara-m pëli-tu.`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>Recipient -tu is noun case, not a verb ending. Preferred repair (if any): `sen mi mara-tu kima-m pëli.`.</details>

9. Evaluate: **`sen mi-na naro-m sëmi-ta.`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>Do not introduce an unattested suffix -ta. Preferred repair (if any): `sen mi-na naro-m sëmi.`.</details>

10. Evaluate: **`më ti haro he ya.`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>he questions and ya requests are different clause acts. Preferred repair (if any): `hë ti haro he?`.</details>

11. Evaluate: **`sen qaro shi.`**
<details><summary>Analysis</summary>This is a valid compound-free two-word phrase in some contexts; diagnose ambiguity rather than force a fake correction. Preferred repair (if any): `sen qaro shi.`.</details>
