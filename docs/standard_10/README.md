# Qaroshi Standard 10.0: Meaning, Conversation, and Independent-Reader Readiness

**Status:** Source-grounded editorial learning and review infrastructure, with no new automatically ratified grammar rules, no new canonical dictionary senses, and **zero independently observed comprehension outcomes**.

This milestone resolves a major product gap: a word page should explain documented senses, show connected speech, and provide practice in the Wiki instead of merely translating one English label. Its purpose is deeper **source traceability**, not inflating attestation figures.

## 9.8: Example and conversation evidence, accurately classified

Existing evidence sources, never replaced or overwritten:

- 4,383 original bilingual teaching entries in corpus/parallel_sentences.csv. Their editorial English is the reference gloss, not an independently established community usage standard.
- 49 original distinct dialogue scripts in corpus/original11/dialogues.json, with 294 turns. These were separately authored as scenes, but they are **not spontaneous or independently speaker-attested conversations**.
- The established source-linked conversation paths in data/v18/source_linked_conversation_paths.json. These recombine existing documented reference lines into additional scenarios. Recombination is NOT evidence of a new natural conversation.
- 1,572 controlled roundtrip bilingual pairs in data/fluency11/translation_pairs.json. These are classroom drills generated by a deterministic translation subset, **not attested real-life sentences or comprehensive meaning proofs**.
- Existing Wiki 9.4 500-case challenge drafts, previously 9.2-corrected as applicable. Their English intent remains source metadata and is **not proof that a reader will independently obtain the same meaning**.
- 5,000 earlier draft sense/example cards from data/editorial/sense_cards_5000.csv. These remain explicitly unreviewed and **cannot** be counted as established additional senses or approved example sentences.

The Wiki now offers **Conversations** with situation/context, click-through original reference words, per-turn revealable contextual and literal meanings, and explicit source labels. **Controlled drills** are a separate searchable, paginated study area, not confused with the original phrasebook or source-authored dialogue collection.

This release does not claim 26,248 missing dictionary examples have become attested sentences. Where verified source-corpus examples do not exist, the 9.7 word page still says so. Creating thousands of unlabeled sentences would be an invalid shortcut.

## 9.9: Actual sense distinctions instead of loose English glosses

Existing source layers are kept distinct:

1. Individual core lexical profiles (data/v12_13/core_lexical_profiles.csv): separately authored explanations, selection restrictions, contrasts and original editorial example forms. These are human-authored *editorial* descriptions, not community ratification.
2. Existing curated senses (data/semantics/curated_senses.json): different meanings and construction types already documented in earlier normative or editorial sources. Preserve their exact inherited source status, including the 24–27 normative clarifications, but do not claim external independent attestation.
3. Priority semantic inventory (data/v28_34/core_semantic_index_800.csv): **800 words**, corpus mention counts, source IDs, previously curated sense links and flags for English gloss overinterpretation. A priority entry is **not itself an approved new definition**.
4. The 5,000 source-gloss selection and example draft cards: visibly **unreviewed** and collapsed behind a disclosure; selected English glosses are not automatically separate polysemous meanings and drafts are not independent translation evidence.
5. All other dictionary entries retain their exact canonical gloss, POS-appropriate grammatical explanation, documented morphology, compounds and sources. Where no individually reviewed semantic definition exists, the Wiki explicitly states this limitation rather than generating speculative senses.

Source compiler: tools/standard10/resources.py. It cross-checks dictionary identity and the original gloss before surfacing profiles or draft cards. Mismatched rows are rejected with a count in the reproducible manifest. No frozen source file is altered.

## Independent-reader validation: the only honest boundary

The generated trial is a real **procedure** for doing future independent human validation, not a fake completed study. It deterministically samples **80 distinct contextual Qaroshi utterances**, with scene limits and two editorial-provenance families. It creates separate files:

- learner_blind_items.csv: Qaroshi-only target plus preceding Qaroshi context and a free-translation task. No English answer appears in the learner sheet.
- facilitator_english_key.csv: original English source target, literal gloss when available, scene and source provenance. Keep away from participating readers.
- empty_response_and_adjudication.csv: zero submitted answers or judgments, including slots for anonymous reader and independent adjudicator.
- PROTOCOL.md: informed consent, exclusion of prior source familiarity, identity-protecting responses, first-pass literal/semantic roles, confidence, key access, ambiguity and later adjudication.
- manifest.json: exact item and provenance counts and a reproducible learner-sheet SHA-256.

The answer key is **separate, not cryptographically secret**: people can read the public source repository. A real blind study must distribute only the learner sheet, control answer-key access, document readers' prior exposure, and obtain manual adjudication. No script or AI run is an independent reader. The scorer rejects empty sheets, unknown IDs, duplicate participant answers, missing paraphrases and unsupported ratings. It cannot authenticate the independence of real people and reports that limitation.

Run from repository root:

    python tools/standard10/resources.py --summary
    python tools/standard10/trial.py --build --out data/standard10/review_pack
    python tools/standard10/trial.py --score private_human_filled_sheet.csv --out private_report.json
    python -m unittest discover -s tests -q
    node tests/standard10/ui.cjs
    make build

The scorer command requires human-supplied answers and adjudicator ratings; without those, it raises an error. GitHub Actions uploads the reader sheet and key as **different artifacts**, retaining the warning about public visibility.

## Standard 10.1: Source contexts, editorial workbench and actual field-study tooling

**New Wiki paths:**

- `#word/mara`: separate **This word in conversation** section, drawn only from exact-matched words or documented suffix forms in original scripted dialogue turns and source-recombined conversation paths. These are **not** counted as examples from the original 4,383-sentence reference corpus, independent attestation or real spontaneous conversations. Each links to its scene, speakers and original text.
- `#editorial`: a local-only review desk for the **800 priority lexical entries**. Search by lemma, gloss and topic; filter missing individual definitions, missing original-corpus examples, existing curated senses, or unreviewed candidate cards. View inherited source meanings, selection constraints and examples before writing editor notes. Download notes in a clearly **UNREVIEWED_EDITORIAL_PROPOSAL_NOT_APPROVED** JSON file. The exported notes change no lexeme and do not count as an independently conducted review. You can also open `#editorial?word=mara` from a priority dictionary entry.
- `#validation`: a documented no-English-key standalone participant study, instead of merely printing a CSV and expecting readers to edit it by hand.

**Generate the reader-only offline study:**

```sh
python tools/standard10/trial.py --build --out trial-export
python tools/standard10/reader_html.py --reader trial-export/learner_blind_items.csv --out trial-export/learner_experiment.html
```

Distribute **only** `learner_experiment.html` to eligible consenting participants. The HTML contains all 80 Qaroshi-only targets and previous Qaroshi context, but **no facilitator English target, semantic verdict, score, network request, browser storage or telemetry**. It requires consent and a locally chosen anonymous code. Respondents write their own first-pass interpretation and roles/scope uncertainty, then export a private CSV. It never marks correctness or reopens the answer key.

**Private human adjudication after collection:**

```sh
python tools/standard10/adjudicate.py \
  --responses reader01.csv reader02.csv \
  --key trial-export/facilitator_english_key.csv \
  --out private_adjudicator_worksheet.csv
# A separate consenting human adjudicator fills adjudicator_anonymous_code,
# semantic_rating and adjudicator_notes in the private worksheet.
python tools/standard10/trial.py --score private_human_completed_worksheet.csv --out private_report.json
```

The adjudication prepper **never** generates a semantic verdict. It validates the original 80 IDs, consent disclosure fields, uncertainty, distinct reader+item response pairs and supplied answer key, and leaves every judgment blank. The existing scorer refuses unrated forms. **Do not upload respondent CSVs, combined worksheets or identifying information to public GitHub.** In particular, the English facilitator answer key is public repository material; you must control its distribution and document prior participant exposure. With zero independent participants, zero human comprehension/attestation results remain the only honest outcome. The Study may be functionally *ready* without being *completed*.

Run `python -m unittest tests.test_standard_10_1 -q`, `make build` and the browser tests to reproduce the safety and data-provenance checks. CI creates the standalone HTML as part of the learner-only artifact, with the answer key in a **separate**, explicitly not-secret artifact.

## What can and cannot be called complete

**Completed in Standard 10:** the existing sense/proposal inventory is usable in the Wiki; all original editorial conversations and existing mechanical drills are discoverable; actual dictionary entries link through source word forms; independent-reader distribution and honest scoring have executable tooling and tests; provenance boundaries are visible.

**Not fabricated as complete:** independent reader trials, native-style attestation, unlimited free translation, community acceptance of the 5,000 draft sense cards, independent semantic adjudication of every dialogue line, or 26,248 newly attested lexical examples.

The path to human validation is now executable, but no human trial result exists until consenting independent people actually participate.
