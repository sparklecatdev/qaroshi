## Why this revision was needed

Earlier automatic English-gloss segmentation proposed false equivalences: musical meter for a water meter, financial tip for tail tip, theatrical mask for protective mask, and abusive grooming for ordinary feather care. These are now individually marked rejected, and broader parenthetical source-sense ambiguity is machine-flagged rather than silently accepted.
