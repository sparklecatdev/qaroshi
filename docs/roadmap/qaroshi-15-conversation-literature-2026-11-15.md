## Authored additions in 15.0

- **73 separately drafted editorial conversations**, **441 turns**, with distinct titles/premises and genres. These were not generated by mechanically swapping nouns in one conversation template; many ordinary, formulaic sentences still recur.
- **12 new editorial prose pieces**, **120 sentences**.
- **561 new aligned Qaroshi/English sentences**, **2,419 Qaroshi-only whitespace tokens**, **312 distinct Qaroshi sentence spellings**.
- Every new sentence passed the v14 documented morphosyntax and basic translation-risk screen, after correction of two doubled direct-object/recipient structures. This is NOT independent semantic or cultural validation.
- Across the previous v11/v12/v13 and current authored text: **176 dialogues**, **35 prose texts**, **1,594 aligned lines**, **6,828 Qaroshi source-language whitespace tokens**. **No speaker-attested source recordings or approved literary translations.**
- The older 1,000 practice dialogue variants / 123,000 template-produced tokens are preserved elsewhere but **excluded** from independent/naturalistic authoring claims.

## Honest linguistic QA

- **347 of 561 new utterance occurrences** belong to repeated-sentence groups; there are **48 exact Qaroshi sentence spellings** with multiple contextual English renderings in new material.
- Across cumulative editorial texts, **223 translation-variation groups** merit review. These may be reasonable context-dependent paraphrases or editorial discrepancies, not automatically grammar errors.
- The source contains lexical/domain breadth in navigation, markets, landscape, gardens, music, social consent, literary discussion, reports, education, and philosophical reflection; its clause syntax remains mainly controlled.
- **Zero speaker attestations or independent semantic approvals.** The 1,000 truly independent conversations and naturalistic 100,000-word corpus targets remain unmet. Pure word counts do not prove naturalness.

- **772 Python tests pass** in development; **772 run on clean extraction with one optional compiled-font test skipped**.
- **18 Chromium desktop/mobile interaction checks pass**, zero page errors.
- Canonical 30,000 lexicon entries, 4,383 reference examples, 90 grammar chapters, 26 native glyph artworks and 16 mandatory written resonances are **byte-for-byte unchanged**, checked via 29 baseline SHA-256 references.
- The 431-file ZIP passes CRC and fresh-extraction/rebuild checks; **font binaries are intentionally excluded**. The full ZIP is a separate conversation deliverable, not part of this branch.
