# Detailed sentence anatomy atlas

Twenty-one source-backed parses selected to show grammar in context. The Qaroshi sentence, its unchanged canonical translation, and the broad IPA are given beside a human-readable morphological analysis. The analyses describe grammatical meaning, not new dictionary entries.

## How to read the atlas

Read each original aloud. Cover the English and state what case and verb endings you can identify. Then check the grammatical analysis and compare the natural English. Finally repeat the line without hyphens to ensure that you do not treat teaching separators as separate native letters.

## 1. Are you well?

**Original:** `hë ti haro he?`
**IPA:** `/hə ti ˈha.ɾo he/` · **Stress:** `hë ti HA-ro he?`
**Analysis:** `hë` inquiry resonance; `ti` second-person subject; `haro` adjective predicate “well”; `he` direct yes/no question ending. No present “are” is required.

**Natural English:** Are you well?

---

## 2. I cherish you deeply.

**Original:** `më mi ti-m nashi.`
**IPA:** `/mə mi tim ˈna.ʃi/` · **Stress:** `më mi tim NA-shi.`
**Analysis:** `më` Warm Hum; `mi` subject; `ti-m` second-person object; `nashi` lexical predicate of deep care. Warm Hum does not prove romance.

**Natural English:** I cherish you deeply.

---

## 3. May I hug you?

**Original:** `hë mi ti-m mërali-ho he?`
**IPA:** `/hə mi tim mə.ɾa.ˈli.ho he/` · **Stress:** `hë mi tim më-ra-LI-ho he?`
**Analysis:** `hë` inquiry register; `mi` subject; `ti-m` object; `mërali-ho` hug + permission `-ho`; `he` asks for permission, not physical ability.

**Natural English:** May I hug you?

---

## 4. Please stay with me.

**Original:** `më pëla mi-wa nëra ya.`
**IPA:** `/mə ˈpə.la ˈmi.wa ˈnə.ɾa ja/` · **Stress:** `më PË-la MI-wa NË-ra ya.`
**Analysis:** `më` affectionate delivery; `pëla` please; `mi-wa` “with me” comitative; `nëra` remain; `ya` request ending. Unlike an English wish, this directly requests staying.

**Natural English:** Please stay with me.

---

## 5. I will protect you.

**Original:** `qor mi ti-m daru-yu.`
**IPA:** `/qoɾ mi tim da.ˈɾu.ju/` · **Stress:** `qor mi tim da-RU-yu.`
**Analysis:** `qor` solemn commitment; `mi` subject; `ti-m` patient; `daru-yu` protect + future. A promise needs its actual content, not just solemn resonance.

**Natural English:** I will protect you.

---

## 6. Please never die, so you can stay with me forever.

**Original:** `më pëla, ti sëna nu zëru ya, ko ti mi-wa sëna nëra-ki.`
**IPA:** `/mə ˈpə.la, ti ˈsə.na nu ˈzə.ɾu ja, ko ti ˈmi.wa ˈsə.na nə.ˈɾa.ki/` · **Stress:** `më PË-la, ti SË-na nu ZË-ru ya, ko ti MI-wa SË-na në-RA-ki.`
**Analysis:** One opening `më` covers both clauses. `sëna nu zëru ya` is emphatic negative request; `ko` introduces the purpose; `mi-wa` with me; `nëra-ki` “able to stay.”

**Natural English:** Please never die, so you can stay with me forever.

---

## 7. I will sleep.

**Original:** `sen mi shërami-yu.`
**IPA:** `/sen mi ʃə.ɾa.ˈmi.ju/` · **Stress:** `sen mi shë-ra-MI-yu.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `mi` subject; `shërami-yu` sleep + future. Time reference is after contextually relevant now.

**Natural English:** I will sleep.

---

## 8. I slept.

**Original:** `sen mi shërami-te.`
**IPA:** `/sen mi ʃə.ɾa.ˈmi.te/` · **Stress:** `sen mi shë-ra-MI-te.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `mi` subject; `shërami-te` sleep + past. Nothing in the suffix alone says whether the event was completed in a special viewpoint.

**Natural English:** I slept.

---

## 9. I was sleeping.

**Original:** `sen mi shërami-te-se.`
**IPA:** `/sen mi ʃə.ɾa.mi.ˈte.se/` · **Stress:** `sen mi shë-ra-mi-TE-se.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `mi` subject; `shërami-te-se` sleep + past + progressive. The event is viewed as ongoing in the past.

**Natural English:** I was sleeping.

---

## 10. I sleep regularly.

**Original:** `sen mi shërami-në.`
**IPA:** `/sen mi ʃə.ɾa.ˈmi.nə/` · **Stress:** `sen mi shë-ra-MI-në.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `mi` subject; `shërami-në` habitual; a recurring pattern is asserted, not necessarily activity right now.

**Natural English:** I sleep regularly.

---

## 11. I drink water.

**Original:** `sen mi kima-m këmë.`
**IPA:** `/sen mi ˈki.mam ˈkə.mə/` · **Stress:** `sen mi KI-mam KË-më.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `mi` subject; `kima-m` water direct object; `këmë` verb drink. Object-before-verb order.

**Natural English:** I drink water.

---

## 12. Nardoragons fly.

**Original:** `sen qaro-ri goro.`
**IPA:** `/sen qa.ˈɾo.ɾi ˈɡo.ɾo/` · **Stress:** `sen qa-RO-ri GO-ro.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `qaro-ri` plural subject; `goro` fly. A general assertion has no overt tense suffix.

**Natural English:** Nardoragons fly.

---

## 13. They fly using wings.

**Original:** `sen sa banu-shi goro.`
**IPA:** `/sen sa ba.ˈnu.ʃi ˈɡo.ɾo/` · **Stress:** `sen sa ba-NU-shi GO-ro.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `sa` subject; `banu-shi` wing + instrument; `goro` fly. `-shi` is means here, not a companion.

**Natural English:** They fly using wings.

---

## 14. We see stars in the sky.

**Original:** `sen naru sëra-ri-m wena-lo tika.`
**IPA:** `/sen ˈna.ɾu sə.ˈɾa.ɾim we.ˈna.lo ˈti.ka/` · **Stress:** `sen NA-ru së-RA-rim we-NA-lo TI-ka.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `naru` inclusive we; `sëra-ri-m` stars plural and direct object; `wena-lo` location in the sky; `tika` see.

**Natural English:** We see stars in the sky.

---

## 15. I'm going toward home.

**Original:** `sen mi naro-gu gura.`
**IPA:** `/sen mi na.ˈɾo.ɡu ˈɡu.ɾa/` · **Stress:** `sen mi na-RO-gu GU-ra.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `mi` subject; `naro-gu` goal toward home; `gura` travel. Do not substitute locative when motion toward is intended.

**Natural English:** I'm going toward home.

---

## 16. I'm leaving home.

**Original:** `sen mi naro-de gura.`
**IPA:** `/sen mi na.ˈɾo.de ˈɡu.ɾa/` · **Stress:** `sen mi na-RO-de GU-ra.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `mi` subject; `naro-de` source away from home; `gura` travel. The contrast to `-gu` changes direction.

**Natural English:** I'm leaving home.

---

## 17. They flew; I saw it.

**Original:** `sen sa goro-te si.`
**IPA:** `/sen sa ɡo.ˈɾo.te si/` · **Stress:** `sen sa go-RO-te si.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `sa` subject; `goro-te` flew; `si` firsthand evidential. This is a past event with witnessed support.

**Natural English:** They flew; I saw it.

---

## 18. They flew, according to a report.

**Original:** `sen sa goro-te hu.`
**IPA:** `/sen sa ɡo.ˈɾo.te hu/` · **Stress:** `sen sa go-RO-te hu.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `sa` subject; `goro-te` flew; `hu` reported evidential. The source changed, not the event.

**Natural English:** They flew, according to a report.

---

## 19. They seem to have flown.

**Original:** `zher sa goro-te ye.`
**IPA:** `/ʒeɾ sa ɡo.ˈɾo.te je/` · **Stress:** `zher sa go-RO-te ye.`
**Analysis:** `zher` uncertain/suspicious stance; `sa` subject; `goro-te` past; `ye` inferential evidential. Stance and source remain distinct categories.

**Natural English:** They seem to have flown.

---

## 20. If I could fly, I would travel into the sky.

**Original:** `sen wënu mi goro-ki, mi wena-gu gura-yu qinë.`
**IPA:** `/sen ˈwə.nu mi ɡo.ˈɾo.ki, mi we.ˈna.ɡu ɡu.ˈɾa.ju ˈqi.nə/` · **Stress:** `sen WË-nu mi go-RO-ki, mi we-NA-gu gu-RA-yu QI-në.`
**Analysis:** `sen` neutral; `wënu` counterfactual premise; `goro-ki` can fly; consequent `gura-yu` will travel; `qinë` marks unreal projected result.

**Natural English:** If I could fly, I would travel into the sky.

---

## 21. May you be safe.

**Original:** `më yëmë ti haro.`
**IPA:** `/mə ˈjə.mə ti ˈha.ɾo/` · **Stress:** `më YË-më ti HA-ro.`
**Analysis:** `më` Warm Hum; `yëmë` optative wish; `ti` subject; `haro` safe/well predicate. This is a wish, not a direct imperative.

**Natural English:** May you be safe.
