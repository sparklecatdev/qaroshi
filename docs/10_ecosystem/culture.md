# Culture and usage in Qaroshi

Qaroshi's written resonance system foregrounds the social intent of each independent statement. Warm Hum `më` signals tenderness; Deep Resonance `qor` frames solemn promises; Seeking Call `hë` frames inquiry; Still Voice `sen` frames neutral reporting. No resonance proves a sentence truthful or makes a request obligatory.

The lexicon contains words for homes and roosts, wings, flight, stars and sky, friendship and chosen family. These can be used literally or in contextual metaphor, but an isolated word does not establish an ancient proverb. `nashi` means cherish/deeply love and does not automatically mean sexual or romantic desire.

No historical timeline or regional dialect is asserted without dated cultural or speaker records. Community contributors should identify provenance and distinguish literary worldbuilding, standardized pedagogy, and witnessed usage. Such honesty preserves the distinction between a coherent language reference and verified linguistic fieldwork.
