# English–Qaroshi translation workflow

This release supplies a **dictionary-assisted translation workbench**, not a general neural translator. For English to Qaroshi, first resolve agent, recipient, time, modality, evidence and intended interpersonal stance. Select documented lexical roots, order arguments using Qaroshi SOV structure, add nominal case and ordered verbal morphology, and prefix a resonance. For Qaroshi to English, decompose prefixes/suffixes, determine evidence/negation scope and translate the entire proposition rather than mechanically preserving word order.

The structural checker can identify missing opening resonance, malformed suffix sequences and some conflicting question/command patterns, but cannot certify a novel translation's truth conditions or the idiomaticity of long prose. Proper names and foreign quotations may legitimately be unknown to the dictionary.

To evaluate translations, maintain a parallel set of complex sentences, require independent translations, and judge tense, scope, number, source-of-evidence, consent/permission and tone separately. A high word count alone is not translation quality.
