# 15.0 natural-fluency completion gate: assessment

## Gate A: 1,000 dialogue transcripts

**Quantitatively achieved; qualitatively not independently validated.** There are exactly 1,000 distinct transcript instances in the cumulative library: 176 separately drafted editorial scenarios inherited unchanged from v11/v12/v13/v15, plus 824 newly constructed scenarios. These 824 use fifteen controlled discourse arcs and sixteen thematic lexical inventories, so **none** is counted as a separate human-independent original work for linguistic-attestation purposes. Their titles, topical noun pairs, source transcripts and formal ordering were checked for exact duplicates; qualitative literary differentiation remains limited.

## Gate B: 100,000-word corpus

**Quantitatively exceeded when procedural material is included, but not the originally intended naturalistic requirement.** The 211,366 Qaroshi graphic-word tokens were counted strictly from the text of Qaroshi utterances in the 1,205-piece cumulative library. We explicitly exclude IPA, translations, titles, glosses and the pre-existing 123,000-token template-based exercise inventory. Only 6,828 of these tokens belong to separately drafted editorial pieces; 204,538 were procedurally composed. 

Distinct exact sentences number 10,415. Repeated sentence occurrences number 43,327 / 47,655 (**90.92%**). Source root variety and genre breadth remain low for an independently naturalistic 100,000-word benchmark. The procedural prose module is a first-person commentary exercise and is not a peer-reviewed story corpus. Reports must not describe it otherwise.

## Gate C: grammar consistency

**Automated surface checks passed, semantic review pending.** Every new sentence was screened with Qaroshi Standard 14.0. It recognized no hard surface errors in this release. An additional **1,988** complex conditional or coordinated clauses carry rule `R14-010` and remain unresolved for scope and semantic naturalness. The data exports preserve all reviewer flags; no automatic correction has changed the canonical dictionary.

## Gate D: speaker evidence / translation quality

**Not met.** There are no independently approved translations, spontaneous conversational transcripts, documented community dialects or consented human pronunciation recordings in this extension. Long-form sense and idiom acceptance cannot be inferred from rule compliance or volume.

## Recommended next exact editorial workflow

1. Have independent writers replace the procedural 824 scripts with bespoke conversations of their own with actual conversational repair, negotiation, uncertainty, jokes and third-party reference. Treat each replacement as a new authored work with traceable provenance, not a renamed template.
2. Write and review diverse genre prose until the independently drafted source corpus itself reaches 100,000 Qaroshi tokens. Track per-genre coverage and narrative completeness, not just size.
3. Resolve 1,988 complex scope review rows, 231 groups of contextual English divergence, and high-frequency repeated expressions before promoting material into the Academy.
4. Collect explicit written license, speaker consent for any authentic recordings, and at least two separate reviewer decisions for meaning and naturalness. Preserve original references and record revisions independently.
5. Run held-out reading/translation tests and publish revised rules with minimal pairs and reproducible comparisons; only then promote reviewed writing into standard evidence.
