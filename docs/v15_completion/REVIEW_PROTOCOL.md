# Corpus quality review protocol

For every candidate passage, reviewers record (1) intended meaning, (2) literal and pragmatic English interpretations, (3) referenced participant for every pronoun, (4) grammatical status of each case, suffix and resonance, (5) whether the sentence sounds appropriate in its discourse situation, (6) authenticity/source status, and (7) a concrete edit or accept/reject rationale. Two independent assessments are preferred for any change proposed as part of the standard.

The automatic validator surfaces candidate issues, **not** independent human judgments. The 1,988 `R14-010` findings indicate a complex coordinator/conditional with unresolved scope, rather than certain grammatical failure. In exactly the same way, a repeated Qaroshi sentence with multiple contextual English renderings does not by itself prove contradictory meanings; interpret the surrounding conversational referents and sense.

Never copy algorithmically constructed text into a "native speaker example" dataset without informed contributions and licenses. A contributor may withdraw unpublished recordings or ask for a correction. Do not collect identifiable private data or inferred dialect attributes via public corpus files.

The offline Corpus Atlas stores personal reviewer notes locally; exporting the JSON does not modify the canonical data. When accepting an editorial correction, add a new revision with source, parent version, reviewer identity/pseudonym and tests, rather than silently overwriting the original wording.
