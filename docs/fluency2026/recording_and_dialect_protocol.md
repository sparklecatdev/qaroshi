# Pronunciation and dialect evidence protocol

## Status
There are **0 consented human word recordings** in this editorial expansion. The 3,000-item CSV is a collection plan, not audio. Browser TTS, phonemic IPA, and easy sound-outs are approximations and must never be labelled native-speaker recordings.

## Speaker consent
A speaker can choose recording, public attribution or pseudonym, license, and the ability to withdraw unpublished material. Record an unambiguous affirmative consent record and a narrow use scope before recording. Minimize personal data: avoid storing legal names, addresses, government IDs, or health/demographic inferences in the public corpus. An optional statement of a self-described dialect is not a verified regional dialect unless supported by many independently documented speakers.

## Technical guidelines
Record a short quiet reference phrase and the target word in context. Prefer lossless 48 kHz PCM WAV master (mono is acceptable), normalize only after storing the unaltered master, and add human-reviewed segmentation/IPA. Associate one recording ID with consent, a checksum, speaker-chosen attribution, context sentence, and review decision. Do not publish raw audio without a clear license. If a recording is rejected, keep only the reasons needed to audit the decision.

## Accents/dialects
Collect ≥3 independently consenting recordings per proposed phonological feature before describing it as a recurring observation; do not call a variant a 'regional dialect' without geographic and community evidence. Preserve spoken variance rather than silently editing it to fit the written standard. Provide a controlled comparison with the phonemic standard and explain uncertainty.

## Quality assurance
Two independent reviewers should verify recorded word identity, the native alphabet transcription, phonetic annotation, file quality and consent. Reject submissions that expose private conversations, minors' identifying details, or unlabeled synthetic speech. Store signed consent references offline or in permission-controlled systems, not in the public GitHub repository.
