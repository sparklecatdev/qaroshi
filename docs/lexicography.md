# Qaroshi dictionary conventions

All 11,878 entries belong to one alphabetically searchable lexicon, regardless of when they were documented. Each headword has a stable ID, a part of speech, an English meaning, a semantic domain and optional usage notes.

## Entries and forms

A headword is an independent dictionary form; a compound combines lexical elements; a function word governs grammar or discourse. Inflectional variants are normally formed through grammar rules rather than counted as new headwords. The English index includes multiple gloss aliases where applicable.

The Romanization uses six vowels and the 26-letter original Nardoragon alphabet. `sh`, `zh`, `kh`, and `ng` each correspond to a single native glyph. Two superficially similar terms are not automatically synonyms.

## Meaning and usage

Definitions identify principal senses, not every contextual nuance or possible translation. Terms for specialist concepts should be interpreted within their semantic domains; unusual expressions may be rendered as explanatory clauses.

## Maintenance

New dictionary entries must have a distinct spelling, a meaningful gloss, a defined word class and a domain. Preserve existing identifiers and document corrections. Corpus examples are teaching material, not a survey of real-life speaker frequency.
