# Language coverage and future reference work

The Qaroshi reference provides broad coverage of everyday conversation and specialized concepts, but no finite dictionary can list every sense or idiom. Further reference work may add authentic dialogue examples, regional variants, semantic distinctions, corpus frequency data, alternate morphological analyses and accessibility features.

Such future work can extend the standard without changing its current dictionary entries silently.


## After 7.0: validation priorities

Formal agreement and morphology are documented; rigorous future work requires speaker review and independent attestation, a much larger connected corpus, corpus-tagged sense frequencies, dialect sampling with consent, audio-backed phonetic descriptions, and automated parse/ambiguity diagnostics. The 7.0 datasets are controlled teaching resources, not fieldwork.
