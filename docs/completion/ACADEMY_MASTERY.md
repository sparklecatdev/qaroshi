# Advanced Qaroshi mastery track

The new **Completion Workbench** includes 94 reference-backed minimal meaning contrasts alongside the earlier 24-lesson textbook, original Academy, and 12 original connected dialogues. The recommended editorial learning sequence is:

1. **Recognition:** interpret the 16 mandatory written resonance registers and the difference between literal meaning and stance.
2. **Nominal structure:** plural, case, possession, reference and the consequences of suffix slot order.
3. **Predicate structure:** past/future reference, progressive/habitual/completive aspect and causative/passive/reciprocal voice.
4. **Modal nuance:** ability, permission, obligation, commitments and uncertainty.
5. **Clause meaning:** negation scope, evidence, quotations, reported content, conditionals and reason/purpose.
6. **Extended comprehension:** read and paraphrase distinct short scenes without revealing English translations initially.
7. **Editorial judgement:** independently label ambiguities and suggest repairs for idioms and sense cards rather than mechanically reciting forms.

The workbench tracks multiple-choice practice accuracy locally. Accuracy is **not** the same as comprehensive fluency or an independent CEFR-style proficiency rating. The learning environment does not provide live pronunciation assessment or human listening evaluation. Its stored answers are exportable as JSON for continuity.
