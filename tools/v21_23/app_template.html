Evidence, limits & next research priorities

Translation

Exact reference matching is dependable as a lookup; contextual English may still paraphrase source meaning. For other inputs this app abstains and suggests related references. A separate rule-based CLI only generates from a curated subset.

Immersion

108 earlier authored/editorial readings are re-presented without rewriting source sentences. Their learning bands are quantitative proxies, not empirically calibrated difficulty or CEFR levels.

Assessment

Quizzes measure the ability to recognize an existing line's editorial English. They are not an independent measure of fluency, open-ended translation, or plot comprehension.

Source fidelity

The original 30,000-entry lexicon, 4,383 example sentences, established grammar, 26 glyphs and 16 resonance markers remain unchanged. A previous ledger of 103 potential translation issues is carried forward as explicitly unresolved until external semantic review.

Human validation protocol

Real learners should read without seeing English, give their own interpretations, and review source disagreements. Record learner consent, prompt, accuracy, dialect claims and reviewer status. This application does not manufacture those results or collect them remotely.

Next roadmap

24: independent reader and speaker review with an issue tracker. 25: speech-to-text evaluation using consented audio. 26: expanded translation once grammar and meanings have been approved against genuine comprehension data.
