Editorial transparency

What is this app?

What it can do

Find exact original teaching phrases with English, citation IPA, sound-out, and source ID; follow contextual scripted dialogues; build a restricted sentence; render the original native glyph art; study high-utility roots; save favorites and study progress locally.

What it cannot claim

It is not a certified real-world speech corpus or general translator. Automated grammar surface validation does not establish naturalness, social appropriateness or dialect authenticity. Editorial dialogue translations can paraphrase rather than reproduce every truth condition. The sentence builder is a narrow controlled grammar drill.

Preservation

The 30,000 canonical lexemes, 4,383 original bilingual examples, 90 grammar chapters, 16 resonance markers and 26 original glyphs remain unchanged. No unapproved v16.2 replacement words have been adopted. No accounts, tracking, network calls, font binaries or human audio recordings are provided.

Next practical gap

Real speakers independently using and correcting these expressions, recorded pronunciation with consent, broad free-form conversation, a tested platform keyboard, and independent semantic adjudication.
