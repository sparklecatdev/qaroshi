Review protocol and compatibility

Entries labeled editorial trial received a source-gloss, collision, sense-boundary, and practical word-length review. This is one editorial screening, not independent community approval. Entries labeled machine shortlist only passed a reproducible heuristic and must not be treated as individually reviewed.

  • Canonical 30,000 lexicon entries and original 26 written glyphs remain untouched.
  • All 16 written resonance markers and the documented morphology remain binding.
  • Every proposal is explicitly separate from accepted lexemes.
  • Shortness and component fit matter, but conversational naturalness is not machine-verifiable.
  • False friends, homophonous-looking collisions, technical senses and overbroad newly coined roots are held or rejected.
  • Local reviewer decisions are saved only in this browser, and can be exported. No analytics or network access.

The repo includes raw datasets, reproducible Python generators, test cases, a full tracked-source preservation manifest, and a clear written backlog for human reviewers.

Not complete: independent speaker testing, semantic acceptance of new forms, independent corpora with actual conventional usage, or an approved new lexical standard.
