What these two versions standardize

18.0 turns the established conversational grammar into testable rules and source-linked dialogue routes. 19.0 reorganizes root learning, makes proposal risk visible, and maintains explicit meaning and collision boundaries. Both are additive educational layers; neither silently changes the canonical lexicon.

Source chain

Original 30,000-word lexicon, 4,383 references, 90 grammar chapters, 16 resonance markers and 26 glyphs. Existing v14-v17 code and data are retained in the source archive.

Review standard

A proposed form may be semantically plausible, misleading, or ambiguous. Do not promote a trial to canonical merely because its two English glosses appear to fit. Speaker results require real independent reviewers.
