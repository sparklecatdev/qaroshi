# Qaroshi 20.0 · Start Here

Open **`Qaroshi_20_Speech_Studio.html`**. It works offline for pronunciation teaching, stress exercises, sound contrasts and search. Microphone recording depends on your browser's permissions and origin security.

**Learn**: 26 sound values, 144 dictionary sound contrasts, 105 stress drills, 110 resonant phrase readings, 140 connected-speech prompts and 14 lesson days. **Record**: choose one of 3,000 source-linked prompts, confirm consent, click Record, then Stop, replay, download audio and metadata. Multiple recordings during one session appear in the session list, but recordings are never silently uploaded or persisted.

**License and authenticity:** These are language teaching resources and private speaker-initiated recordings. Zero human voice samples have been generated, recorded, or distributed by this release. The 30,000-word canonical dictionary, 4,383 bilingual reference sentences, 90 grammar chapters, 16 written resonance markers and 26 native glyphs are unchanged. All new paths are additive and preserve contributor work from earlier versions.

Run `python tools/v20/build_release.py` to rebuild. See `docs/v20/SPOKEN_STANDARD.md` for the full technical/consent protocol and `data/v20/release_manifest.json` for exact counts.
